Innovation & R&D Culture

Paint & varnish R&D powered by global know-how.

İBAVALRESA R&D, nourished by global know-how, develops technological products and innovative formulations; it creates value-adding, application-focused solutions that put customer needs at the center.

01 — Our Perspective

Technology-Focused Product Development.

In our R&D work we develop technological products that increase application efficiency and speed up processes. Our formulations, which optimize curing times and offer high durability and surface quality, meet aesthetic performance and industrial requirements together.

With water-based systems and low-VOC solutions, we offer safer products for both applicators and end users.

The products we develop bring together high performance, aesthetic surface quality and global quality standards.

02 — Our Perspective

Innovation with Sustainability.

Sustainability sits at the heart of our innovative product-development approach. In our R&D processes we aim to reduce environmental impact, use resources more efficiently and create long-term solutions.

We develop our products with a focus on sustainable and eco-friendly technologies.

03 — Our Perspective

Design and Application Solutions.

We develop solutions for the varied application needs of the furniture and wood industry. Our products offer not only performance but also ease of application and visual quality.

Our R&D approach is not limited to product development; it also works actively on different application techniques and process compatibility, and on field solutions that add value — directly contributing to our customers' production processes.

From the lab to the field, we turn knowledge into solutions.
