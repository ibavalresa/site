On request, RAL, NOVA and NCS colors are produced at our factory with Valresa Color System color machines.

RAL Color Chart

The RAL color chart is a reference system of standardized color numbers and codes used internationally. Developed by the German Color Association, it is widely used to ensure color consistency, especially in furniture paints and varnishes. It covers thousands of shades, each identified by a unique RAL code — for example, RAL 9010 represents pure white and RAL 5002 a blue tone.

NCS Color Chart

NCS (Natural Color System) is an international color-standardization system that defines and classifies colors according to attributes such as hue, saturation and brightness. It expresses colors objectively to ease communication; each color is represented by a code combining numbers and letters — for example, S 2060-Y10R represents a yellowish tone and S 3050-B90G a green close to blue. It simplifies color selection and harmony in furniture and interior design.

Download NCS PDF Chart

NOVA Color Chart

The Nova color chart is a color-reference system developed specifically for the furniture industry. It offers standardized color codes across a range of shades, guiding manufacturers and designers in color selection. It lets users easily identify and communicate specific colors, ensuring consistent and accurate color presentation in furniture products.
