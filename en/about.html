About Us

A Turkish–Spanish manufacturer of wood and industrial paints & varnishes.

İBAVALRESA Boya ve Kimya A.Ş. was founded in Ankara in 2005 by İBA Kimya A.Ş. and VALRESA SA as a Turkish–Spanish partnership to design, manufacture and market wood and industrial paints and varnishes.

Partnership Structure

A strength born from a Turkish–Spanish partnership.

Türkiye + Spain

İba Kimya Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş., which began paint and varnish production in Ankara in 1977, added powder coating facilities in 1995 and became a leading producer in Türkiye's powder coating sector.

Valresa SA began producing furniture paints and varnishes in Valencia, Spain in 1965, and opened its second facility in Mexico in 2004. Valresa-branded paints reach 6,000 customers across 5 continents and are among globally recognized brands.

İba Kimya and Valresa SA, each a leader in its sector in different countries, combined their production know-how and experience to found İBAVALRESA Boya ve Kimya A.Ş. in Ankara in 2005, further strengthening their position in the industry. The company operates under the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System.

Powered by this partnership, our exports have today reached around 50% of total sales; under the Valresa brand we now represent the Türkiye arm of a global network reaching more than 6,000 customers across 5 continents.

What We Do

End-to-end solutions in wood and industrial coatings.

Since 1977 we have been a manufacturer specialized in wood paints and varnishes and industrial coatings. We are among the leading producers in Türkiye's wood coatings market.

  • Field of Activity: Wood paints and varnishes, General Industrial Coatings
  • Product Range: Acrylic, Polyurethane, Cellulosic, Water-based, UV, Polyester, Epoxy systems. Solutions for both interior and exterior applications.
  • Brands: Valresa, Esfera, İbavet, İba Industrial Coatings
  • Market Position: Among the largest producers in Türkiye's wood coatings market
Campus & Facility

A modern production campus in Ankara Sincan.

At our modern facility built on 37,000 m² in the Ankara Sincan, we operate with an annual production capacity of 30,000 tons. Our strong production infrastructure is supported by ISO 9001:2015 and SAP-integrated process management.

  • Founded: 1977, Ankara – Türkiye
  • Production Facility: Ankara Sincan – 37,000 m²
  • Annual Production Capacity: 30,000 tons
  • Employees: Approximately 150
  • Quality Management: ISO 9001:2015, SAP-integrated quality and process management

Our History

Milestones.

1977

Production of insulation chemicals, thinners and solvents began.

1979

Wood paint production began and consumers were introduced to İBAVET-branded products.

1980

The majority shares of VOLKAN Paint, an industrial paint producer, were acquired by the DAL Family.

1984

Alkyd resin production began.

1998

İBA Kimya A.Ş., which decided to continue in the electrostatic powder coating sector, transferred its furniture paint, alkyd and thinner production and marketing activities to the newly founded İBAVET Boya ve Vernik Ltd. Şti.

1999

A quality assurance system was established and the ISO 9001:1994 certificate was obtained.

2002

The ISO 9001:2000 Quality Management System certificate was renewed.

2005

A joint investment with Valresa (Spain) in wet-paint operations was decided and İBAVALRESA was founded. Construction of the new wet-paint facilities began.

2007

A new facility on a 32,000 m² site in the expansion area of the 1st Organized Industrial Zone in Ankara Sincan was commissioned and trial production began.

2010

The first exports were carried out.

2015

SAP was integrated as the ERP system. The ISO 9001:2008 Quality Management System certificate was renewed.

2018

The ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certificate was renewed.

2023

With a solar power plant investment, renewable energy generation began.

2024

With a 5,000 m² land addition, the facility area was increased to 37,000 m².

2026

Logistics capacity was strengthened with an ASRS automated storage and retrieval system.
