Wood, Furniture & Industrial Coatings Manufacturer in Turkey

With the production and R&D strength born from our Turkish–Spanish partnership, we deliver high-quality wood coatings, furniture varnishes and industrial coating solutions to global markets.

İBAVALRESA is a Türkiye (Turkey)-based wood coatings manufacturer, producing acrylic, polyurethane, water-based and UV-curing furniture finishes for both domestic and export markets. Turkey is among the world's leading producers and exporters of wood coatings and furniture varnishes.
