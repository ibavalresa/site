Quality Management & Certificates

İBAVALRESA Boya ve Kimya A.Ş. carries out all its activities within the framework of the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System. Our products are tested in independent laboratories and certified to international conformity standards.

Quality is the value that shapes our entire corporate culture, guides our processes, forms the basis of the trust we offer our customers and feeds our continuous-improvement mindset — our most fundamental value.

Our Approach

Our Quality Management Approach

Our understanding of quality is not limited to the products we manufacture; it forms the basis of all our activities — from the service we offer our customers to our production processes, and from our supply chain to our continuous-improvement efforts. In line with the ISO 9001 Quality Management System we have held for many years, we manage our processes in accordance with international standards, keeping customer satisfaction and sustainable development at the forefront.

To provide reliable products and services to our customers, we:

  • We effectively apply the requirements of the ISO 9001 Quality Management System and continuously improve the performance of our system.
  • We set measurable quality targets, monitor our performance regularly and evaluate the results we achieve.
  • Through internal and external audits carried out regularly, we verify the effectiveness of our processes and turn identified improvement areas into opportunities.
  • We regularly review the effectiveness of our quality management system through Management Review Meetings, process performance assessments and continuous-improvement efforts.
  • We continuously improve our processes by systematically tracking customer feedback, complaint management, risk and opportunity assessments, and corrective and preventive actions.
  • In line with our digital-transformation vision, we deploy new infrastructures, software and management tools that support our quality management system, making our processes more effective, traceable and sustainable.
  • We encourage the active participation of all our employees in quality processes and embrace quality awareness as an integral part of our corporate culture.

We see our Quality Management System not as a structure consisting merely of documents and procedures, but as a living management system that measures, analyses, audits, digitalises and continuously evolves. Thanks to our culture of continuous improvement, we build more efficient processes every day and continue to offer our customers reliable, high-performance and sustainable products.

Because for us, quality is not merely a standard that is reached, but a management approach that is improved every day.

Our Policies

We place customer satisfaction, continuous improvement and environmentally conscious production at the center of our understanding of quality. All our employees share a common responsibility to reach the defined quality goals.
