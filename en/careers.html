İBAVALRESA Careers

A team that grows together.

At İBAVALRESA, we stand by our employees at every career stage — from internship to full-time work, from new graduate to experienced professional — connecting their knowledge, experience and skills with the right tools and development opportunities.

Students Project Engineers Job Opportunities Our Employees Integrity & Ethics
Student Internship Program

We provide high-school and associate-degree students at our partner schools with long-term, practice-oriented internships that let them get to know working life closely.

We evaluate the mandatory-internship requests of relevant undergraduate students, and in the summer we support their professional development with quality internship programs. We stand by our interns at every stage of their journey so they can turn theoretical knowledge into practice and start their careers better equipped. To this end we run protocol-based collaborations with schools and universities; we see school–university–industry cooperation as an important value for raising the talents of the future.

* Our internship quotas fill quickly, mostly within the allocations reserved for our partner schools. That said, we also have limited quotas for other applications. In this context, an application to our Human Resources department is required for mandatory-internship requests to be considered.

Project Engineers

We offer final-year or master's students part-time roles in our projects for a defined period. Through this, we provide work experience to students who are eager to develop themselves and want a strong start to their careers.

Project engineers who take part in this program also gain the opportunity to be given priority for suitable positions that may arise in the future.

Job Opportunities

We offer new graduates and experienced professionals a variety of career opportunities suited to their education, experience and personal skills. In our recruitment we assess candidates' knowledge, experience and competencies with the right tools, aiming for sustainable placements that fit mutual needs and expectations.

Our focus in recruitment and in the development journeys of our current employees is on competencies defined to fit our company structure, working culture and understanding of success. We care about building a work environment where employees can apply their strengths in the right areas, develop their potential and make long-term contributions.

Our Employees

After hiring, our orientation programs, structured around the needs of the department you will join, help you adapt quickly and easily. Thanks to our knowledge-sharing work environment and the importance we place on digitalization, you can more easily reach the information, support and guidance you need while settling into your new role.

With a principle of continuous development, our employees' training needs are tracked; each employee's growth is supported with different trainings and practices.

You become part of a team where you can develop yourself in the light of science and technology and take your career to higher levels in a happy and warm work environment.

Our Values

Our Integrity and Code of Ethics

To reach our goals, we value having employees eager to do their work within a framework of leadership, fairness and honesty. We are aware of the responsibility we carry toward one another and our customers.

We are the guardians of our brands and their reputation; we do our work properly and honestly to defend the values they represent in the market. We support an open communication environment where we can exchange ideas and information without fear of negative feedback.

As İBAVALRESA employees we place the utmost value on integrity and accountability; we support one another to ensure all our practices are transparent, honest and ethical.

We grow together, take responsibility and add value.
