Sustainability

At the heart of our Environment, Occupational Health and Safety policy lies the goal of leaving a livable world for future generations. Grounded in environmental responsibility, employee health, a safe working environment and a sustainable production approach, we plan and resolutely implement practices that set an example for the chemical industry.

01 — Environment

Environmentally friendly production.

As İBAVALRESA Boya ve Kimya A.Ş., we act with the awareness that production affects not only today but also the future; we build our environmental policy on protecting natural resources, reducing environmental impact and full compliance with legal obligations.

At every stage of our operations we adopt an environmentally conscious, responsible and sustainable production approach, and we care about ensuring the continuity of eco-friendly practices.

  • Enerji ihtiyacının %70'i güneşten karşılanmaktadır
  • Kimyasal atıkların tamamı lisanslı firmalarla bertaraf edilir
  • Paperless production and circular economy practices
  • 1000'den fazla bitkiden oluşan yeşil kampüs
02 — Health

Human health and environmental impact control.

Within our Occupational Health and Safety approach, protecting employee health and keeping environmental impacts under control are among our core priorities. Accordingly, risks to human health and the environment are carefully assessed in our processes, and the necessary preventive measures are resolutely implemented.

Preventing process waste from mixing into soil, water and air; not using chemicals harmful to human health and the environment or banned from use; and managing restricted chemicals within defined limit values form the core elements of this approach. Full compliance with legal requirements and relevant regulations is the basis of all our processes.

03 — Occupational Safety

Employee safety first.

Potential hazards our employees may be exposed to in our production processes are identified through regular risk assessments, and the necessary preventive measures are put in place. We see employee safety not merely as a legal obligation but as a fundamental part of how we work.

To this end, hazard markings and the precautions to be taken are clearly stated on product packaging, and personal protective equipment is selected in accordance with European standards. In addition, periodic trainings on OHS, environment, emergencies and fire are held, and regular emergency drills are carried out to stay prepared for potential risks.
